# What is a Website?

A website is a collection of web pages that are hosted on a web server and accessed through the internet using a web browser. It provides information, services, or interactive content to users and is identified by a domain name.

# Types of Websites

- Informational websites: Provide facts, articles, and resources about a topic or organization.
- Business websites: Showcase company information, products, and services.
- E-commerce websites: Allow users to buy and sell products online.
- Blogs: Publish regular posts or articles on a specific subject.
- Portfolio websites: Display work samples, projects, and personal achievements.
- Social media websites: Enable users to connect and interact with each other.
- News websites: Share current events, updates, and journalism.
- Educational websites: Offer learning materials, courses, and tutorials.
- Entertainment websites: Provide videos, games, music, and other media content.
- Forum websites: Allow users to discuss topics and ask questions in a community format.
