# What is Google Business and How to List Your First Business on Google

## What is Google Business?
Google Business (Google Business Profile, formerly Google My Business) is a free tool from Google that helps businesses manage how they appear in Google Search and Google Maps.

With Google Business, you can:
- Share your business name, address, phone number, and website.
- Display opening hours and business categories.
- Add photos, business descriptions, and services.
- Collect and respond to customer reviews.
- Help people find your business online.

## How to List Your First Business on Google
1. Open your web browser and go to: https://www.google.com/business
2. Sign in with a Google account. If you do not have one, create a new Google account.
3. Click on "Manage now".
4. Enter your business name.
5. Choose the business category that best fits your business.
6. Add your business location so customers can find you on Google Maps. If you do not have a physical location, select "No" when asked if you want to add a location customers can visit.
7. Enter your contact details: phone number and website (if available).
8. Verify your business. Google may ask you to verify by mail, phone, email, or instant verification, depending on the business type and region.
9. Once verified, update your profile with business hours, photos, and a description.

## Tips for a Good Google Business Listing
- Use accurate and consistent information.
- Add high-quality photos of your business or products.
- Respond to customer reviews politely and promptly.
- Keep your hours and details up to date.
- Add services or products if applicable.

Listing your business on Google helps improve visibility and makes it easier for customers to find you online.
